# Which environmental data do you need to collect for EcoVadis?

## In brief

[EcoVadis](https://resources.ecovadis.com/whitepapers/ecovadis-ratings-methodology-overview-and-principles-2022-neutral) environmental indicators measure the company's impact on the planet within the **Environment** theme. For an SME the difficulty is not finding the figures — they already exist in invoices, the carbon footprint and regulatory declarations — but **choosing the most defensible source** when they diverge, and keeping the same method year after year: EcoVadis looks at the trend.

## What does the Environment theme cover?

- **Energy consumption and GHG:** energy use, greenhouse gas emissions (Scopes 1, 2 and 3).
- **Resources and water:** water use, raw materials management.
- **Waste and discharges:** waste production, wastewater and hazardous substances management.
- **Local impact:** biodiversity and local pollution.

## Which indicators, in which units?

| Indicator | Unit |
|---|---|
| Total energy consumption | kWh |
| Total renewable energy consumption | kWh |
| Total water consumption | litres |
| Recycled and reused water | litres |
| Air pollutants | metric tonnes (t) |
| Hazardous waste | kg |
| Non-hazardous waste | kg |
| Recovered waste | kg |

## Which trade-offs do you settle before uploading evidence?

Three points to settle before uploading anything to the platform:

- **Natural gas** — the supplier invoice (451,043 kWh) and the regional environmental declaration (702 MWh) diverge by 36%. The invoiced figure is the most defensible as evidence.
- **Photovoltaics** — the January audit indicated ~38,120 kWh; the supplier's June readings give 43,387 kWh. Keep the readings.
- **Waste** — the carbon footprint counts only 351.88 t (collectors' scope) versus 579.44 t in the regional declaration. Different scopes, to be harmonised.

Two data points usually remain to be produced for full coverage: reused water (no recycling in place → answering "0" is acceptable and documentable) and the historical detail of water consumption, missing from the invoices provided.

## Key takeaways

- The figures almost always exist already: energy invoices, meter readings, carbon footprint, regional environmental declarations, waste collectors' registers.
- When two sources diverge, keep the most defensible one (the invoice rather than the estimate) and document the choice.
- Keep the same method year after year: EcoVadis assesses the trend as much as the value.
- A documented zero beats a missing indicator.
