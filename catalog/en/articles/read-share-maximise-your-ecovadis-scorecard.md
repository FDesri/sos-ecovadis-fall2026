# How do you read, share and maximise your EcoVadis rating?

## In brief

**Expert insight — François Dequenne:** your [EcoVadis](https://resources.ecovadis.com/whitepapers/ecovadis-ratings-methodology-overview-and-principles-2022-neutral) scorecard isn't just a rating. It's your sustainability strategy mapped for customers, investors, and continuous improvement. For many suppliers the EcoVadis score is now a commercial requirement rather than a "nice to have" — yet the scorecard can feel opaque the first time you see it. This article explains how to read it, how to share it, what it costs, how long it is valid, and what a "good" score actually looks like.

## How do you read your scorecard?

The result of an assessment is a scorecard from 0 to 100 with detailed performance across the four themes: Environment, Labor & Human Rights, Ethics, and Sustainable Procurement. At the top you see your **overall score**, your **percentile** (how you compare with all other assessed organisations), and your **medal** if you rank in the top 35% or higher.

Beneath that, each theme is built on the **Policies–Actions–Results (P-A-R)** framework:

- **Policies:** formal commitments — policies, codes of conduct, certifications
- **Actions:** programmes, procedures, training, implementation evidence
- **Results:** KPIs, performance trends, audit results, external reporting

A strong score usually shows a balanced profile: clear policies, robust actions, measurable results in at least the most material themes for your sector. When reading your scorecard insights, focus first on: themes scoring significantly lower than the others; themes with the highest impact on your overall score; themes that have gone down since your last assessment.

## What makes a good EcoVadis score?

A "good" score depends on your sector, your customers' expectations and your maturity, but widely used thresholds are:

| Score | Reading |
|---|---|
| Below 45 | Basic or emerging system, often partial coverage and limited results |
| 45–64 | Structured system, usually acceptable to many customers, with clear improvement needs |
| 65–84 | Strong system backed by evidence and results; often in medal range |
| 85+ | Leading practice; aligned with best-in-class expectations |

Medals are awarded by percentile (and absence of major controversies): Bronze = top 35%, Silver = top 15%, Gold = top 5%, Platinum = top 1%. Commercially, many multinationals treat Silver and above as a strong positive signal in supplier selection, especially in high-risk categories.

## How long is your scorecard valid?

**12 months from publication.** After that, customers may be notified the score is no longer current, you receive reminders to relaunch, and some buyers treat an out-of-date scorecard as non-compliance. In some cases (higher scores, lower risk profiles) customers accept a 24-month cycle — but EcoVadis itself considers the scorecard valid for 12 months, so customer requirements decide. Reassess at least annually if sustainability is part of your commercial proposition, you are working towards specific targets (net zero, human rights due diligence), or key customers actively track your score.

## How do you share your scorecard?

Sharing is a commercial opportunity, not administration. Three primary methods:

- **Direct platform sharing:** accept customer requests or proactively invite key buyers through the EcoVadis secure network
- **Secure view-only links:** let stakeholders explore your scorecard insights and corrective action plan
- **PDF exports:** for tenders, sustainability reports, sales collateral, investor communications

Strategic practices that maximise impact: internal alignment across procurement, sales and sustainability teams for consistent messaging; proactive storytelling that pairs the rating with your corrective action plan, especially on weaker themes; enabling sales and key account managers to articulate how the rating meets procurement requirements and reduces audit burden; and multi-channel amplification — medals on website, proposals and ESG reports, contextualised with industry benchmarks.

## What and when do you pay EcoVadis?

EcoVadis runs an **annual subscription**: the fee covers one assessment per year, platform access and your scorecard, charged when you launch or renew. Higher tiers add functionality (more connected customers, advanced analytics, multi-site options). Treat the subscription as an ongoing investment, embedded in the ESG budget, supplier qualification costs, or frameworks tied to customer obligations. From ESGIM's experience, companies get the best value when they plan the year around the assessment date, using the months before launch to close gaps rather than rushing low-quality evidence.

## How do you read the scorecard's structure to improve?

Beyond the headline figures: theme-by-theme scores with sector and region comparisons; a colour-coded Policies-Actions-Results breakdown; a list of **strengths**; and a list of **improvement areas** that forms the basis of your corrective action plan.

Tips for efficiency: start with your strongest theme to understand what "good" looks like in your own context; then examine the weakest theme, especially if material to your sector (Environment for manufacturing, Labor & Human Rights for labour-intensive chains); use the improvement areas as a roadmap — each bullet usually corresponds to a specific missing or weak document, practice or result.

## How do you improve your score?

> "Every improvement area on your scorecard points to a specific gap: a missing policy, a process not yet formalised, a KPI not yet tracked. Working through that list systematically is how you build a sustainability management system — not just improve a score."

Strong performers consistently do three things: **close evidence gaps** (every relevant policy, procedure and certification up to date, clearly worded, properly uploaded); **strengthen implementation** (documented training, audits and processes that show real deployment); **measure results** (KPIs, targets, year-on-year change in areas such as energy use, diversity, supplier audits).

Typical quick wins: publishing or updating a code of conduct or policy in line with international standards; formalising existing practices (health & safety training, supplier screening) into documented procedures; starting to report basic metrics you already track informally.

> "The organisations that compound their EcoVadis score year on year share one habit: they treat the scorecard as a 12-month management tool. The improvement area list from one cycle becomes the action plan for the next."

## Key takeaways

- The scorecard = overall score, percentile, medal, and a P-A-R breakdown per theme; valid 12 months.
- Reference bands: <45 basic, 45-64 structured, 65-84 strong, 85+ leading.
- Share strategically: platform, view-only links, PDFs — paired with your corrective action plan.
- Plan the year around your assessment date; the improvement-area list is next cycle's action plan.
