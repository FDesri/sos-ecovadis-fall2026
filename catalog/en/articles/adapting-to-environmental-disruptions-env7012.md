# What actions against external environmental disruptions? (ENV7012)

## In brief

ENV7012 extends the climate risk assessment (ENV7003) into the territory of **actions**: what does the company concretely do to withstand environmental disruptions? Two families of evidence are expected — adaptive practices integrated into operations, and emergency reserves or safety nets. No impact on the score, but visible on the scorecard.

## How do you integrate adaptive practices into operations?

**Examples of documents to attach:**

- Operating protocol for extreme weather events
- Drought management plan
- Emergency response plan for external environmental disruptions
- Business continuity plan

**Your document must** demonstrate that the company has adapted its operating processes to manage or mitigate the effects of environmental disruptions, and describe the activities that guarantee service continuity during severe climate or environmental disruptions. Examples of operational adaptations:

- Adapting production schedules or outdoor work during heatwaves or storms
- Scaling down water-intensive manufacturing or cooling processes to keep operating during a local water shortage
- Adapting processes or site maintenance to counter the loss of local ecosystem services (soil erosion, degradation of natural storm buffers)
- Modifying internal logistics routes or access roads to avoid flood-prone areas

## Which emergency reserves and safety nets should you plan?

**Examples of documents to attach:**

- Business interruption insurance certificate
- Safety stock procedure
- Report on the allocation of emergency funds
- Emergency equipment inventory

**Your document must** demonstrate that the company has emergency measures in place to face environmental disruptions and recover from them: mechanisms that preserve financial stability and keep essential services running during or immediately after a severe climate event, notably regarding water supply. Examples:

- Business interruption insurance policies covering natural hazards
- Emergency liquidity reserves for environmental disasters
- Safety stock of critical operating supplies to avoid immediate shutdowns during an external shortage
- Backup operating equipment: generators, temporary flood barriers

## Key takeaways

- ENV7012 is the "actions" side of climate resilience; ENV7003 is its "risk assessment" side.
- Two families of evidence: operational adaptive practices, and reserves/safety nets.
- Many SMEs already have these elements without naming them this way — business interruption insurance, buffer stock, a generator. The job is mostly to document them.
- No impact on the score, but visible on the scorecard — and therefore to your buyers.
