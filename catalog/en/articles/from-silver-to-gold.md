# What does it take to be a top EcoVadis performer? From Silver to Gold

## In brief

**Expert insight — François Dequenne:** at Gold, the question is: are those systems genuinely working, fully deployed, and visible to the outside world? That's a meaningful shift — and it's where I see many Silver holders stall. The companies that break through to Gold consistently do a few specific things differently: SMART, quantitative targets, not just qualitative commitments; a public sustainability report complying with a recognised standard — not just internal reporting; documented measures for every activated criterion, and more. As of May 2026, Gold sits at 81/100, up from 77 just a year ago — moving faster than any other medal level in the first half of 2026. Silver holders with scores in the low-to-mid 70s have less margin than their percentile position suggests, and the 8-point gap between Silver and Gold is not evenly distributed across the scorecard. Knowing exactly where to focus makes the difference.

## What does Gold represent?

The Gold Medal is reserved for the **top 5%** of all companies assessed by [EcoVadis](https://resources.ecovadis.com/whitepapers/ecovadis-ratings-methodology-overview-and-principles-2022-neutral) in the last 12 months. In 2024 that meant a score around 73/100; as of May 2026 it means approximately 81. Reaching it requires meeting and exceeding the criteria for Bronze and Silver and taking a much more detailed approach to sustainability management. It demands time and investment — and it represents a major step in stakeholder engagement and in driving your sustainability strategy forward.

## What does consistent and comprehensive actually mean?

Given the much stricter criteria at Gold level, the key phrase throughout the assessment is **"consistent and comprehensive."**

For Bronze or Silver, complete evidence for all activated criteria is not necessary. For Gold, you need relevant evidence for **as many activated criteria as possible**. There is still room for some gaps — but not in the activated criteria most relevant to your business. Take the time to **triage your activated criteria**: identify where your evidence gaps are and whether they sit in the areas most material to your organisation.

For example: a dairy farm cooperative lacking evidence for its biodiversity strategy should focus its effort there. A manufacturer must provide solid evidence on its health and safety strategy. And for most companies aiming for Gold, adequate evidence of a **proactive engagement strategy with suppliers, customers and other stakeholders** is critical.

## What counts as Gold-standard evidence?

The right supporting evidence is vital. It includes codes of conduct, sustainability reports, records of recent external audits, and training materials — all sufficiently **recent, relevant and complete**.

One type of evidence is essential for both Gold and Platinum: **evidence of effective reporting**. Your material should demonstrate that you have set quantitative goals and have an achievable plan in place for meeting them.

## How do you monitor and measure progress effectively?

Show the frameworks you use to monitor progress: KPI dashboards and policy documents describing your monitoring practices. Include KPIs for stakeholders in your **value chain** — suppliers and customers — to show you address your company's impacts beyond your own gates.

## How do you demonstrate leadership commitment?

Senior management involvement improves the assessment for two reasons. First, Gold requires more financial and time investment than Bronze or Silver: management buy-in must be ongoing all year round, sustained through targeted training and regular strategy update sessions. Second, Gold requires demonstrating that sustainability management is **embedded in business strategy**: executives participating in industry sustainability forums or conferences show that commitment; an integrated annual report with a dedicated sustainability statement documents it. Working this way, your EcoVadis score improves as the organisation's sustainability maturity grows.

## Key takeaways

- Gold = top 5% of all rated companies; approximately 81/100 as of May 2026, up from 77 a year earlier — the fastest-rising medal level in early 2026.
- The watchwords are "consistent and comprehensive": evidence for nearly all activated criteria, with no gaps in the most material ones.
- SMART quantitative targets and a public sustainability report to a recognised standard separate Gold candidates from Silver holders.
- KPI dashboards should extend to the value chain: suppliers and customers.
- Leadership commitment must be visible, budgeted and embedded in business strategy.
