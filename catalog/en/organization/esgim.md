# ESG Interim Management (ESGIM) — who are we?

## In brief

ESG Interim Management, or **ESGIM**, is a Belgian collective of interim managers specialised in sustainability. We take charge of a company's EcoVadis journey end to end, the way an interim manager takes charge of a business unit: on site, with the teams, through to the agreed result. Our scope is deliberately narrow — very small (XS) and small (S) companies, in Belgium and the Benelux — because that is where the gap between what is done and what is proven is widest.

## What does ESGIM actually do?

Three things, in this order. We audit the gap between the company's real situation and what EcoVadis expects. We build what is missing: policies, measures, indicators, registers, documentary evidence. We submit the questionnaire and follow the assessment through to the agreed level.

What sets us apart fits in one sentence: the final instalment is due only if the agreed level is reached.

## Who do we work for?

Companies of fewer than a hundred people that receive an EcoVadis request from a large customer, want to move up a medal, or have just been downgraded. Most frequent sectors: manufacturing, chemicals, printing, special building techniques, transport and logistics, food.

## Where do we operate?

In Belgium — Wallonia, Flanders and Brussels — and across the Benelux. Deliverables are produced in French, Belgian Dutch or English, depending on the client's working language.

## Who writes this catalogue?

François Dequenne, EcoVadis Interim Manager at ESGIM, is its author and reviewer. His expert entry sets out his background. Every published entry carries his name, its last revision date and its version.

## How do you reach us?

By email at **fd@esgim.eu**. The full presentation of the collective and the profiles of all its experts are on [esgim.eu](https://esgim.eu/).
