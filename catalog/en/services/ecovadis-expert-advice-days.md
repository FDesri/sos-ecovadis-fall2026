# EcoVadis expert analysis and advice: 1 to 3 remote days

## The problem it solves

In upgrade situations (S1) there is usually already someone internally in charge of EcoVadis. What the company lacks is not arms but **SME-specific expert judgement**: which levers actually move the score, in which order, at what cost.

## What the service is

**1, 2 or 3 days of remote expert analysis and advice.** The ESGIM expert reviews your scorecard, your corrective action plan and your evidence, and returns a prioritised, budget-realistic set of recommendations — the ones that move an XS/S company's score most efficiently.

## Price

| Package | Price excl. 21% VAT |
|---|---|
| 1 day | **€1,500** |
| 2 days | **€2,850** |
| 3 days | **€3,750** |

If it turns out a full support mission is needed in an upgrade situation, it is priced at €9,000 (XS) or €10,000 (S) — see the [pricing sheet](../pricing/how-much-does-ecovadis-support-cost.md).
