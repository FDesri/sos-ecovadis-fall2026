# Full EcoVadis support: from audit to the agreed medal

## The problem it solves

Your company faces an EcoVadis assessment — a first request from a large customer (S3), a downgrade to recover from (S2), or a medal to move up (S1) — and has neither the time nor the internal resources to run it properly.

## What the service is

**End-to-end support**: audit of your current position, action plan, creation of the documents and evidence, submission of the questionnaire, and follow-through **up to the agreed level (Bronze or Silver)**. Not remote advice: a seasoned ESGIM expert works on-site, alongside your teams, as an interim manager.

## Deliverables

- Gap audit against your (previous) scorecard and the activated criteria
- Prioritised action plan the company can actually commit to
- Policies, procedures and evidence documents, created with each department
- Questionnaire completed and submitted on the EcoVadis platform
- The agreed medal — or continued support at no further cost through to the next assessment 12 months later

## Duration and price

Two to four months. €7,500 to €12,000 excl. 21% VAT depending on size (XS/S) and situation (S1/S2/S3) — the full grid and the three-instalment schedule are in the [pricing sheet](../pricing/how-much-does-ecovadis-support-cost.md).
