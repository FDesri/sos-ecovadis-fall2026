# Must companies submit life cycle assessments (LCAs) for good governance scoring on CDP?

Full LCAs are **not mandatory for all products**, but conducting LCAs or similar analysis on your main products is advised to support the governance score. Concentrating on core offerings balances resource use and aligns with CDP expectations — a focused analysis of what you actually sell most beats a thin attempt to cover the whole portfolio.
