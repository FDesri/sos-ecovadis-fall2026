# Does the 2026 materiality pilot influence the score, and how should companies use it?

EcoVadis launched a **materiality customisation pilot in early 2026** for a limited number of sectors. Within those sectors, companies can — based on their materiality assessments — indicate that a specific environmental topic is not material to their operations. If accepted, those questions are **deactivated and removed from the assessment scope**, which adjusts what the company is scored on.

The pilot is still in early rollout: coverage is limited, with no further public update from EcoVadis on its expansion since January 2026. For eligible companies, the process requires a **formal, documented justification** — a standalone double materiality assessment following a recognised methodology (GRI materiality process or CSRD double materiality) is the most credible approach, including stakeholder-engagement evidence, the methodology, conclusions per topic and governance sign-off. Analysts review the justification, so the rigour matters. For companies outside the pilot sectors the mechanism is not available — check eligibility directly with EcoVadis before investing time in this route.
