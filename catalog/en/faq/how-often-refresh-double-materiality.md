# How often should companies refresh their double materiality assessment?

Double materiality assessments should be **updated regularly and thoroughly documented**. Auditors often review DMA processes to verify stakeholder selection, threshold-setting, and the treatment of negative human-rights impacts. Companies that incorporate insights transparently and demonstrate an evolving understanding of their material topics tend to have smoother assurance processes — a static, one-off DMA is a red flag.
