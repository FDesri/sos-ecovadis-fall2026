# What practical steps can cultivate employee engagement in emissions monitoring?

Communication and education underpin cultural change. Organisations should **embed carbon-reduction goals in strategy and leadership communication, assign accountability**, and use tools such as sustainability task forces. Even small firms can begin measuring Scope 1 and 2 emissions incrementally to build awareness and data maturity — the act of measuring is itself an engagement lever.
