# What makes a high-quality Ethics policy, and which controls should be documented?

A high-quality Ethics policy demonstrates: **clear anti-corruption, anti-fraud, anti-money-laundering and anti-bribery commitments with specific prohibited behaviours listed**; information security and confidentiality provisions; **conflict-of-interest procedures with disclosure requirements**; grievance and whistleblowing mechanisms; and **explicit application to subcontractors and third parties**. The policy should cover the entire organisation, carry management approval and a date, and ideally name the legislation it aligns with — the UK Bribery Act, FCPA, or Sapin II.

What separates a high-quality Ethics policy from a standard one is **specificity**: describing the due diligence process applied to third parties and outlining how non-compliance is handled.
