# We are not subject to CSRD — what levers ensure our sustainability work is recognised by EcoVadis?

[CSRD](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2464/oj) compliance is **not a prerequisite** for a strong EcoVadis score. Companies outside [CSRD](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2464/oj) scope have several effective routes. The **[VSME](https://www.efrag.org/en/projects/voluntary-reporting-standard-for-smes-vsme/concluded)** (Voluntary SME Standard, developed by EFRAG) is now formally recognised by EcoVadis as a reporting framework: the Basic Module achieves up to **75/100** in the Reporting indicator, and the full [VSME](https://www.efrag.org/en/projects/voluntary-reporting-standard-for-smes-vsme/concluded) (Basic and Comprehensive) can reach **100/100**. **[GRI](https://www.globalreporting.org/standards/)** reporting remains highly effective and accessible. Without any public report, the maximum achievable Reporting score is **50/100**. Endorsements such as **[UN Global Compact](https://unglobalcompact.org/participation/report/cop)** and **Science-Based Targets** commitments add score independently of regulatory status.
