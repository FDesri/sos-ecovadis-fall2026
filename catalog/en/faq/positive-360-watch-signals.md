# What counts as a positive signal in the 360° Watch — and can you plan for it?

The [360° Watch](https://ecovadis.com/) operates primarily as a **modifier**: its neutral position is 75/100 per theme, and findings adjust it up or down. Positive scores above 75 are possible but **rare**. EcoVadis monitors external public sources — news media, NGO reports, recognised certifications, industry endorsements — and an upward adjustment typically requires **multiple credible, independent sources confirming positive ESG outcomes**.

What qualifies: widely recognised sector sustainability awards confirmed in independent media; independently verified ESG certifications outside the standard criteria set; sustained positive coverage of specific initiatives across multiple independent outlets; verified impact data cited in credible external sources. What does not: marketing communications promoting a sustainable product range without independent verification, and **press releases issued by the company itself**. Articles by partners or NGOs may qualify if they reference specific, verifiable outcomes.

EcoVadis does not publish explicit criteria for positive [360° Watch](https://ecovadis.com/) scoring — which is why ESGIM does not advise planning or investing in it as a primary lever. The more reliable path remains strengthening the Policies, Measures and Reporting indicators.
