# What is the best way to document and present a significant CO₂ emissions reduction?

A measurable CO₂ reduction — say a 28% decrease in electricity-related emissions versus the prior year — can support several sections at once.

**For the Reporting indicator**: the reduction should appear in your sustainability or annual report as a **quantified KPI**, expressed as an absolute or percentage figure with a clear reference year and scope boundary (Scope 1, Scope 2 location-based or market-based). **For the Measures indicator**: the reduction evidences an implemented action — the document should describe **what was done** (renewable-energy procurement, an efficiency investment, a process change), not just the outcome: a project summary, energy audit report or internal management document with dates and scope. **For the Coverage indicator**: state explicitly what percentage of operations the action applies to — a 28% reduction across 100% of electricity consumption is very different from one site.

An internally created KPI dashboard works well, provided it carries the **company name, a date, and clearly covers the reporting period**.
