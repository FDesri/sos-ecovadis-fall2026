# Is the reassessment anniversary date the deadline to submit, or only to start?

The anniversary date is **not your submission deadline**: it is the date your current medal **ceases to be valid**. The medal is valid for exactly one year from the date [EcoVadis](https://resources.ecovadis.com/whitepapers/ecovadis-ratings-methodology-overview-and-principles-2022-neutral) published your scorecard — published on 18 June 2025, valid until 18 June 2026. Your submission deadline, if you want an up-to-date medal without a gap, sits **several weeks earlier**.

How far in advance depends on your situation: if you always need a valid medal for customer or tender purposes, aim to submit the new questionnaire **one to two months before the anniversary** — during busy periods the review process can take up to **three months**, so factoring in that window is safer. The key question: what happens if there is a gap between your old medal expiring and a new one being issued? If a customer contract or tender qualification is at risk, submit early; if a short gap is tolerable, there is more flexibility. Plan backwards from the outcome you need.
