# What role do risk assessments and monitoring systems play in climate resilience?

Companies need to integrate **climate risk analysis and emissions data in an iterative monitoring system**. Sector-specific protocols — especially in finance and insurance — guide the focus on transitional and physical risks. Such systems support better investment decisions, compliance, and resilience planning. For [EcoVadis](https://resources.ecovadis.com/whitepapers/ecovadis-ratings-methodology-overview-and-principles-2022-neutral), documented external climate-risk assessment also feeds specific Environment questions (see the ENV7003/ENV7012 articles).
