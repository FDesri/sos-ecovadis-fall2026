# During a reassessment, are previously rejected or unanswered questions flagged automatically?

Previously rejected documents are **not flagged automatically** in the questionnaire interface, but two tools in the platform help you work through this systematically. The first is the **Corrective Action Plan** from the previous scorecard, which identifies improvement areas and signals where evidence fell short or was missing. The second is the **Analysis of Your Answers** section — accessible by scrolling down in your published scorecard — which shows the status of each submitted document for each question, including whether it was accepted. Reviewing it before the reassessment quickly reveals which questions carried rejected documents, which had no evidence, and which can simply be resubmitted.

Combining both — the CAP for high-level priorities, the Analysis of Your Answers for document-level detail — gives the most complete picture of where to focus in the new cycle. **Unanswered questions** (excluding those answered negatively, e.g. "No" or "Don't know") **are flagged directly in the questionnaire**.
