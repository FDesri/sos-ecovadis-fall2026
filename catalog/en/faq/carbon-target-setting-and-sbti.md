# How should companies approach carbon target setting, especially SBTi commitments?

Setting science-based targets **signals ambition, integrity and leadership** to investors and customers. Some firms hesitate over achievability, but SBTi targets help organisations clarify reduction pathways and gain stakeholder trust. The emphasis should be on **credible baselining and progressively ambitious commitments** — a defensible starting inventory matters more than a spectacular headline number. In EcoVadis terms, a validated science-based target is high-quality evidence of a quantitative GHG commitment.
