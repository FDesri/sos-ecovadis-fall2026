# What does "complies with reporting standards" actually require — is GRI "with reference" enough?

EcoVadis scores the Reporting indicator on a **tiered basis** depending on the level of alignment with a recognised standard: **no public sustainability report → capped at 50/100**, regardless of KPI quality; **[GRI](https://www.globalreporting.org/standards/) "with reference to" → as of April 2026, qualifies for up to 75/100**, provided other quality criteria are met (a meaningful improvement from the previous methodology, where "with reference" earned a Strength but no specific score); **[GRI](https://www.globalreporting.org/standards/) "in accordance with" / [ESRS](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2464/oj) compliance / [VSME](https://www.efrag.org/en/projects/voluntary-reporting-standard-for-smes-vsme/concluded) adoption → the route to maximum Reporting scores of up to 100/100**.

For reports in transition: EcoVadis scores **what is demonstrably published at the time of submission** — there is no "in transition" scoring tier, and a partially developed report cannot be submitted as evidence. If a [GRI](https://www.globalreporting.org/standards/) "with reference" report can be published before submission, that is preferable to an unaligned report or none at all.
