# How do questions sitting in multiple themes affect scoring — do they carry increased weight?

Some evidence items contribute to **more than one theme simultaneously**: a supplier Code of Conduct, for example, may count toward both Ethics (anti-corruption provisions) and Sustainable Procurement (supplier sustainability requirements). This cross-theme benefit is one reason comprehensive documents carry more weight than narrow, single-topic ones.

The weighting mechanics depend on the indicator type: Reporting indicators follow the same weighting formula across all pillars, while endorsements and certifications may carry different weights depending on the pillar. Questions spanning multiple themes do **not automatically receive increased weighting within a single theme** — the multiplier effect comes from strong cross-theme evidence improving multiple theme scores at once, not from any single indicator being scored more heavily.
